About Kathleen Kingen · Dahlia Joy Creations

About Me

Hi, I'm Kathleen Kingen, the owner and maker behind Dahlia Joy Creations. What began as a longtime quilting hobby gradually grew into something more. I've been quilting for over 25 years and began longarm quilting in 2020. What I love most about the creative process is choosing fabrics, working with color, and turning those choices into something beautiful, useful, and meaningful.

Based in Edmonds, Washington, I offer longarm quilting services along with a variety of handmade and thoughtfully designed items, including quilts, rope bowls, embroidered pieces, 3D-printed décor, and seasonal gifts. Longarm quilting is an especially important part of Dahlia Joy Creations, and I enjoy helping other quilters bring their finished quilt tops to life.

The name Dahlia Joy is personal to me as well — it comes from the middle names of my daughter and me. That connection reflects much of what inspires my work: creativity, family, and the joy of giving something handmade.

Dahlia Joy Creations is a small, family-supported business. I personally handle the quilting, sewing, embroidery, and handmade creations, while my husband helps with our 3D-printed designs and creations. Together, we take pride in creating pieces that are made with care, creativity, and personal attention.
