Longarm quilting guide

How to Prepare Your Quilt

A few simple steps before quilting can help your quilt load smoothly and finish beautifully.

A little preparation goes a long way

Before dropping off or shipping your quilt to Dahlia Joy Creations, please review the preparation guidelines below. If you have a question or your quilt does not quite meet one of these requirements, contact me. We can discuss the best option.

Before quilting

Preparing your quilt top

Your quilt top represents a great deal of time and work. A few finishing steps before quilting can help everything go more smoothly on the longarm.

✓

Press your quilt

Your quilt top does not need to be freshly pressed, but it should be reasonably flat and ready to load onto the frame. Seams do not need to be pressed open, but they should be pressed flat.

✓

Clip loose threads

Trim loose threads from both the front and back of your quilt top. Pay particular attention to dark threads behind light-colored fabric, since they can sometimes show through after the quilt is completed.

✓

Check that your quilt is square

Measure your quilt across the top, middle, and bottom. These three measurements should be reasonably close to one another. If there is a significant difference, your quilt may have excess fullness or may not finish completely square after quilting.

✓

Check your seams

Look over your quilt top for open seams, gaps, loose stitching, or unsecured seam ends. Repair these areas before quilting. If pieced edges, bias edges, or seams extend to the outside edge, please stay-stitch approximately 1/8 inch from the edge across them to help prevent seams from opening during loading.

✓

Mark the top

If your quilt has a definite top or is directional, please clearly mark the TOP with a safety pin, Post-it note, removable label, or other temporary marker. Please mark the top of both the quilt and the backing.

✓

Remove embellishments

Please remove any pins, safety pins, basting stitches, buttons, charms, beads, and other dimensional embellishments that could interfere with quilting. These can be added back after quilting.

✓

Keep it clean

Please make sure your quilt is clean and reasonably free of pet hair, smoke odor, lint, or other debris. A clean quilt helps ensure the best possible finished result on the longarm frame.

If your quilt has borders or areas with fullness, waves, puckering, or out-of-square areas, please let me know when you submit your quilt. Long-arm quilting can help distribute minor fullness, but it cannot always eliminate excess fabric, puckering, or other construction issues.

Size matters

Quilt backing and batting

Properly sized backing and batting are necessary for securely loading your quilt onto the longarm.

📏

Please allow 5 extra inches on every side

Both the backing and batting must extend at least 5 inches beyond the quilt top on all four sides. Add 10 inches to the width and 10 inches to the length of your quilt top.

Example
40" × 50" quilt top

50" × 60" backing and batting

More is fine. Less may prevent the quilt from being properly loaded. Dahlia Joy Creations uses a rod-and-snap system, so the extra backing and batting provide the working space needed to securely load and position your quilt.

Preparing your backing

  • Clearly mark the top of the backing.
  • If your backing requires piecing, sew the backing pieces together before bringing or shipping your quilt.
  • When possible, leave selvages on the outside edges of your backing.
  • For selvages inside a backing seam, remove the selvage or clip through it periodically.
  • Make sure backing seams are secure and pressed flat.
  • Make sure the backing is reasonably square and even so it can be loaded properly onto the frame.
  • Keep the backing clean and reasonably free of pet hair, smoke odor, lint, or other debris.

Preparing your batting

You may provide your own batting or select batting through Dahlia Joy Creations. If you are supplying your own, make sure it is at least 5 inches larger than the quilt top on every side, clean, free of debris, smoothly folded, and appropriate for machine quilting.

If batting will be supplied by Dahlia Joy Creations, we can determine the right option for your quilt.

Borders and fullness

Ideally, the quilt and its borders should lie reasonably smooth without significant waves, puckers, or excess fabric.

If your quilt top has fullness, puckers, or wavy borders, I will do my best to work with them during quilting. However, these issues cannot always be quilted out completely, and some tucks or puckers may remain.

Excess fullness in borders can also affect the final shape of the quilt and may result in corners that are not perfectly square.

For the best result, your quilt top should lie as flat and smooth as possible before quilting.

If you are struggling with borders, it may be worth reviewing a few border-application tutorials before bringing in your quilt.

✕

Please don't baste your quilt

Your quilt top, batting, and backing are loaded separately onto the longarm machine. Please do not:

  • Pin-baste your quilt
  • Spray-baste the layers together
  • Hand-baste the layers together
  • Sew the quilt top, batting, and backing together

Bring or send the three layers separately. This allows each layer to be properly positioned and tensioned on the longarm frame.

Getting it here

Drop-off or shipping

  • Any special instructions
✈

Shipping your quilt

Place your quilt top, backing, batting, and binding materials inside a clean plastic bag or moisture-resistant wrapping before putting them into a sturdy shipping box. Include a note with your name, return address, phone number, email address, and any special instructions. Choose a shipping service with tracking and appropriate insurance.

Please complete your intake form before shipping. Shipping instructions and address will be provided once your intake form has been reviewed.

Before you send your quilt

  • Quilt top pressed
  • Loose threads clipped
  • Seams checked
  • Quilt reasonably square
  • Top marked if directional
  • Embellishments removed
  • Quilt clean and free of debris
  • Backing at least 5 inches larger on every side
  • Batting at least 5 inches larger on every side
  • Quilt is not basted
  • Backing top clearly marked
  • Contact information included if shipping
