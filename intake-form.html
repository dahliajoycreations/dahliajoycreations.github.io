Long-arm quilting intake

Welcome to Dahlia Joy Creations!

Hi, I'm Kathleen, and I provide professional long-arm quilting services through Dahlia Joy Creations. I know how much time, care, and work goes into creating a quilt top, and my goal is to make the quilting process simple, organized, and comfortable from start to finish.

This intake form helps me collect the information I need about your quilt before quilting begins. You'll be asked for details such as your quilt size, contact information, quilting preferences, thread and batting choices, and any other information that may help me understand how you would like your quilt finished.

Once you submit your intake form, I will review the information and contact you if I have any questions or if we need to discuss any of your selections. We will confirm the quilting details, pricing, and arrangements for getting your quilt to me before work begins.

I currently offer edge-to-edge long-arm quilting, with a variety of quilting designs and density options available to complement your quilt.

Please take your time completing the form. If you are unsure about a question or would like help choosing a quilting design, thread, batting, or other option, that's completely fine — we can make those decisions together.

Thank you for trusting me with your quilt. I look forward to helping you give it a beautiful finished touch.

Kathleen

What to expect

Turnaround expectations

My current turnaround time is approximately two weeks from receiving your quilt and payment, provided all quilting details have been confirmed.

Turnaround time may vary depending on the time needed to finalize your quilting selections, including the quilting pattern, thread choice, and quilting density, as well as any unexpected issues with the quilt such as fullness, waviness, puckering, or other conditions that may need additional attention.

If I expect your quilt to take longer than approximately two weeks, I will contact you with an updated estimated completion date.

You will receive status updates throughout the process, including when:

  • Your quilt has been received
  • Quilting has begun or is in process
  • Quilting has been finished and your quilt is ready for pickup or has been shipped back to you

My goal is to keep you informed throughout the process while giving your quilt the time and attention it needs for a quality finish.

Shipping your quilt

If you are shipping your quilt to Dahlia Joy Creations, please complete your intake form before sending your package. The shipping address will be provided after your intake form has been completed and reviewed.

Before packing your quilt, make sure you have included everything needed for your selected services:

  • Your completed quilt top
  • Quilt backing
  • Batting, if you are providing your own
  • Prepared binding or binding fabric/materials, if you have selected a binding service
  • Any other materials we have discussed for your project

Please keep the quilt top, backing, batting, and binding materials separate unless otherwise instructed.

Protecting your quilt during shipping

Place your quilt and all accompanying materials inside a plastic bag or other moisture-resistant wrapping before placing them in the shipping box. This provides additional protection in case the package is exposed to rain, spills, or other moisture during transit.

Use a sturdy shipping box that is appropriately sized for your quilt and securely seal the package before shipping.

Tracking & insurance

I strongly recommend using a shipping service that provides tracking so you can monitor your quilt while it is in transit.

You may also want to consider purchasing shipping insurance based on the value of your quilt and materials. Quilts can represent a significant investment of both time and materials, and shipping insurance can provide additional protection in the event your package is lost or damaged during transit.

Please keep your tracking number and shipping receipt until your quilt has been confirmed as received.

Once your quilt arrives, you will receive a status update confirming that it has been received.

Disclaimers

Quilting construction disclaimer

I take great care with every quilt and will make every reasonable effort to return your quilt flat, square, and free of puckers or pleats. However, long-arm quilting cannot always correct issues caused by fullness, waviness, uneven borders, stretched fabric, open seams, or out-of-square construction.

If significant fullness or other construction issues are present, some puckering, tucks, or pleats may occasionally be unavoidable. If I identify a significant concern before or during quilting, I will make a reasonable effort to contact you so we can discuss available options before proceeding.

By submitting your quilt for service, you acknowledge that some construction-related imperfections may remain despite reasonable care and professional quilting practices.

Fragile, vintage & specialty quilt disclaimer

Vintage quilts, delicate fabrics, hand-stitched pieces, weak seams, embellishments, and other specialty materials may be more susceptible to damage during normal handling and quilting.

Please identify any fragile, damaged, repaired, embellished, or unusual areas before quilting begins. I will handle your quilt with reasonable care; however, Dahlia Joy Creations cannot be responsible for damage caused by pre-existing deterioration, weak seams, fragile materials, hidden defects, or embellishments.

If I discover a significant concern, I will make a reasonable effort to contact you before proceeding or performing any additional chargeable repairs.
