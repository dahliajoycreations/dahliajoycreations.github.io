Pricing

Longarm quilting rates

Simple, honest pricing.

Longarm quilting is priced by the square inch of your quilt top. This makes it easy to know what to expect before your quilt ever reaches the frame.

The main service

Edge-to-edge quilting

$0.04 – $0.06 per square inch

$100 minimum quilting charge

An all over pantograph pattern quilted across your entire quilt top. The final rate depends on the density of the pattern you choose. Lower density designs sit at the lower end of the range; higher density designs sit at the higher end. Browse quilting patterns ↗

Estimate your project

How pricing is calculated

Multiply the width by the length of your quilt top to get the total square inches, then multiply by the pattern rate.

Example — 60" × 80" quilt, low-density pattern
Quilt width60 inches
Quilt length80 inches
Total square inches4,800 in²
Rate$0.04 / in²
Estimated quilting cost$192 + applicable sales tax

Thread

$10 per quilt

I keep a selection of high-quality quilting threads from Fil-Tec (Glide) in a variety of colors so we can choose a thread that complements your quilt.

Common questions

Pricing FAQ

How much does longarm quilting cost?

Edge-to-edge longarm quilting is $0.04 to $0.06 per square inch of the quilt top, depending on pattern density, with a $100 minimum. For example, a 60" × 80" quilt (4,800 square inches) with a low-density pattern is about $192 plus applicable sales tax.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes. A $100 minimum applies to all edge-to-edge quilting.

Do you offer custom quilting?

Edge-to-edge quilting is offered at this time. Custom quilting is not currently offered.

Can I supply my own batting or thread?

Customer-supplied batting is welcome, or batting can be provided. Thread comes from my own Fil-Tec Glide inventory for a flat $10 per quilt; customer-provided thread is not used.

Do you accept mail-in quilts?

Yes. Quilts are welcome from across the United States. Return shipping is a flat $24 regardless of quilt size. Local drop-off in Edmonds, Washington is by appointment. See How to Prepare Your Quilt for packing and shipping details.

What is the turnaround time?

Turnaround is quoted at the time your project is scheduled.
